Dream Pet

An AI pet with an inner life. Your pet gets bored, wanders off to read the web about whatever it's curious about, and at night turns what it read into a dream, every scene traceable to a page it really read.

01Drives

It does things because it wants to

Nobody tells the pet to explore. Boredom creeps up while it's awake, and once it passes 0.66 the pet goes off to read. Every satisfying page pulls boredom back down, and the session ends once it's satisfied or has read its fill. Searching and reading cost energy, and sleep brings it back. Bedtime is 22:00, and once a night it dreams.

02Curiosity

Curiosity you can measure

Before opening a page, the pet writes down what it expects the page to say. How far the guess lands from the real thing is its surprise. It enjoys moderate surprise most, because a page it predicted perfectly teaches nothing and a page it can't make sense of is just noise. Topics where its guesses keep getting better pull it back, and reading the same topic again pays a little less each time.

03Dreams

Then it dreams about it

At night the pet picks the memories that mattered, wanders from each to related-but-different ones, and weaves a short surreal dream. A critic sends drafts back if any scene isn't built from a real memory, or if more than 12 words in a row are copied from a source. Then a screenwriter turns the dream into a 10-second film script, ready to render with a video model.

Pip's dreams follow its reading. A week before The Sovereign's Ghost it was dreaming about why the sky is blue, then it wandered via Lord Rayleigh, who explained the blue sky and sat in on séances, to web security and the Latin root of the word itself. A different pet, reading different things, dreams something else entirely.

04Personality

Personality is wiring, not a vibe

Each pet comes with its own personality, and every trait shapes how it behaves. It even shifts a little each night, based on what it read. Here's how Pip is set up:

  • restlessnessboredom grows 1.39× faster
  • openness16% chance of a wildcard topic
  • depthfavours its most promising topics
  • sociabilitymessages you first, up to 4 a day
  • stamina120 energy, 17.4 an hour back asleep
  • dreaminessdreams at temperature 0.9, 3 hops wide
